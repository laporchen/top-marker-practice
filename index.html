Press one of the Start buttons to hand out the debuffs and start the timer.

Mark a player with the hotbar slots on their row: the Attack slot gives them the next attack marker, the Stop slot gives them the next stop marker. Each slot shows the number it will give next; a highlighted slot means that player already has that marker, and pressing it again removes it.

Keyboard still works: hover a player and press 1 for attack, 2 for stop, 3 to remove.

The timer stops when all the correct markers are placed. Hover a rules switch to see who should be marked.
